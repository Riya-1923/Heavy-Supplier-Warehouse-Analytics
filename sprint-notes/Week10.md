# Week 10 – BI & KPI Integration Dashboard

## Sprint Objective

The objective of Week 10 was to consolidate the analysis completed during Weeks 1–9 into a single interactive Business Intelligence dashboard using Power BI.

Rather than creating separate dashboards for each analytical area, the key results from Inventory, Warehouse, Supplier, Procurement, Customer, and Predictive Customer Analytics were integrated into one dashboard.

## Work Completed

### 1. KPI Integration

Integrated major KPIs developed throughout the project, including metrics related to:

* Inventory
* Warehouse operations
* Supplier performance
* Procurement
* Customers
* Customer value
* Customer risk

### 2. Business Visualizations

Created interactive visualizations to analyze:

* Inventory performance
* Warehouse performance
* Supplier performance
* Procurement activity
* Customer segmentation
* Customer purchasing behaviour
* Customer risk
* Estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* Regional and branch-level performance
* Year-wise trends

### 3. Interactive Slicers

Added dashboard filters for:

* Region
* Branch
* Product Category
* Supplier
* Customer Type
* Customer Segment
* Risk Level
* Year

These slicers allow users to dynamically explore different business segments.

### 4. Customer Risk & Predictive Analytics

Integrated the predictive customer analysis developed in Week 9.

The dashboard includes:

* Estimated CLV
* Customer risk level
* High-risk customers
* Very high-risk customers
* High-value customers at risk
* Customer value analysis

### 5. Detailed Analysis

Included detailed tables for:

* Customer information
* Supplier information
* Business performance metrics

### 6. Business Insights

Added a dedicated section for summarizing important observations from the integrated dashboard.

The insights cover:

* Inventory trends
* Warehouse efficiency
* Supplier performance
* Customer behaviour
* Customer value
* Customer risk
* Regional performance
* Business trends

## Dashboard Deliverable

A single integrated Power BI dashboard was created as the final Week 10 deliverable.

**Dashboard File:**

`Week10_BI_KPI_Integration_Dashboard.pbix`

## Sprint Outcome

Week 10 completed the project by bringing together the analytical work performed during Weeks 1–9 into a centralized BI dashboard.

The final dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance and allows users to explore Inventory, Warehouse, Supplier, Procurement, Customer, and Predictive Customer Analytics using common filters and visualizations.
