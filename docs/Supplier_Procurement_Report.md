# Supplier & Procurement Analytics Report

## Objective

Evaluate supplier contribution, reliability, procurement activity, delivery performance, and supplier dependency.

## Datasets Used

- suppliers.csv
- purchase_header.csv
- purchase_lines.csv
- products.csv

## Analysis Performed

### Supplier Contribution

Calculated procurement value and purchase order count for each supplier.

### Supplier Reliability

Analyzed the reliability score provided in the supplier dataset.

### Purchase Order Analysis

Analyzed purchase order status and procurement values.

### Delivery Performance

Compared expected delivery dates with received dates to identify on-time and delayed purchase orders.

### Supplier Dependency

Calculated each supplier's share of total procurement value.

### Product-Supplier Analysis

Identified the number of products supplied by each supplier and suppliers associated with critical products.

## Key Findings

The analysis identifies:

- High-value suppliers
- Reliable suppliers
- Suppliers with delivery delays
- Suppliers with high procurement dependency
- Suppliers providing critical products

## Recommendations

- Monitor low-reliability suppliers.
- Review suppliers with delivery delays.
- Reduce excessive supplier dependency.
- Maintain alternative suppliers for critical products.
- Regularly monitor supplier KPIs.
