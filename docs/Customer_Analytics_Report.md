## 5. Customer Purchase Behaviour

Sales order data was connected with customer information using `customer_id`.

Customer-level purchasing metrics were calculated using sales order information.

The main metrics were:

### Frequency

Frequency represents the number of unique sales orders associated with a customer.

### Monetary Value

Monetary value represents the total `grand_total` generated by a customer.

### Average Order Value

Average order value represents the average sales order value generated by a customer.

These metrics help identify customers based on their purchasing activity and contribution.

---

## 6. RFM Analysis

RFM analysis was performed using three customer behaviour metrics.

### Recency

Recency measures the number of days since the customer's most recent order.

The latest order date in the sales dataset was used as the reference date.

A lower recency value indicates that the customer purchased more recently.

### Frequency

Frequency represents the number of unique orders placed by the customer.

A higher frequency indicates more repeated purchasing activity.

### Monetary

Monetary represents the total customer revenue based on the `grand_total` of sales orders.

A higher monetary value indicates a greater contribution to sales revenue.

---

## 7. RFM Scoring

Customers were assigned scores from 1 to 5 for each RFM component.

### Recency Score

Recency scoring was reversed because more recent purchasing behaviour is more favourable.

### Frequency Score

Customers with higher purchase frequency received higher scores.

### Monetary Score

Customers with higher monetary contribution received higher scores.

The three scores were combined to create:

- `R_score`
- `F_score`
- `M_score`
- `RFM_score`
- `RFM_total`

---

## 8. Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into five segments based on their RFM total score.

### Champions

Customers with the highest combined RFM scores.

These customers demonstrate strong purchasing behaviour across recency, frequency and monetary value.

### Loyal Customers

Customers with strong overall purchasing behaviour but lower RFM scores than the Champion group.

### Potential Customers

Customers showing moderate purchasing activity and potential for further engagement.

### At Risk

Customers with weaker recent purchasing behaviour and/or lower overall RFM scores.

These customers can be monitored for possible retention opportunities.

### Inactive Customers

Customers with the weakest overall RFM scores.

These customers can be considered for re-engagement analysis.
