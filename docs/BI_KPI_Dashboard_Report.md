# BI & KPI Integration Dashboard Report

## Overview

Week 10 focused on creating **one integrated Power BI dashboard** combining the major findings and KPIs from Weeks 1–9.

The dashboard brings together:

* Inventory Analytics
* Warehouse Analytics
* Supplier & Procurement Analytics
* Customer Analytics
* Predictive Customer Analytics

## Dashboard Features

The single dashboard includes:

* KPI Cards
* Business Charts
* Key Business Insights
* Customer Details
* Supplier Details
* Estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* Customer Risk Analysis
* High-Value Customers at Risk
* Interactive Slicers

### Slicers

* Region
* Branch
* Product Category
* Supplier
* Customer Type
* Customer Segment
* Risk Level
* Year

## Key KPIs

* Total Inventory
* Inventory Value
* Warehouse Performance
* Supplier Performance
* Total Customers
* Estimated CLV
* Average Estimated CLV
* High/Very High Risk Customers
* High-Value Customers at Risk

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas

* Jupyter Notebook
* Power BI

## Final Outcome

The Week 10 dashboard consolidates the complete project into **one interactive BI dashboard**, providing a centralized view of inventory, warehouse, supplier, procurement, customer, and predictive customer performance.
