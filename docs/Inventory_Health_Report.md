# Inventory Health & Turnover Report

## Objective

Evaluate inventory performance, identify fast- and slow-moving products, analyze inventory value, and support better inventory management decisions.

## Analysis Performed

- Inventory valuation
- Inventory turnover analysis
- Fast-moving product analysis
- Slow-moving product analysis
- Dead stock detection
- Inventory aging
- ABC (Pareto) classification
- Reorder and safety stock analysis

## Key Findings

- Calculated total inventory value.
- Identified products with high and low sales movement.
- Detected dead stock products.
- Classified products into A, B, and C categories.
- Highlighted products below reorder and safety stock levels.

## Recommendations

- Replenish products below reorder level.
- Monitor Class A products closely.
- Reduce excess stock for slow-moving products.
- Improve demand forecasting to minimize dead stock.
